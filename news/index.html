November 19, 2026

Woodinville is asking what its 1.9 miles of Eastrail should become, and the survey closes Monday

The corridor runs from Wilmot Gateway Park through downtown to the city limits and would tie into the regional Eastrail, the Sammamish River Trail and the future Centennial Trail extension.

Bothell's new Downtown Subarea Plan had its public hearing Tuesday; the council is scheduled to decide December 8

It replaces the 2009 plan with a 20-year plan for downtown and folds four downtown land-use categories into one.

Bothell's 2027–2028 budget, $479.1 million across all funds, was on Tuesday's agenda for final adoption

The General Fund is $180.5 million.

Bothell Fire becomes part of the Shoreline Fire Department on March 1, 2027

Initial results show voters approved Proposition 1; King and Snohomish counties still have to certify.
