Bothell & Woodinville · One useful thing

the drain on your street

November 19, 2026 · Bothell & Woodinville Dispatch, issue 1 · All Bothell & Woodinville news

Leaves on a storm drain grate are how a rainy night turns into water across the street. Bothell maintains over 8,200 public storm drains, and its Adopt-a-Drain program asks you to check one near you at least once a month: rake or sweep the leaves off the grate from the curb, put them in the yard waste cart, and never lift the grate. Sign up at wa.adopt-a-drain.org; a drain still clogged after cleaning goes to Public Works at 425-488-0118. City of Bothell
