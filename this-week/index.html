Heads up

  • Birchman Woods, 3407 and 3415 228th Street SE: comments on a 23-lot plat close Monday, November 30, at 5 p.m. File SUB2026-29377; written comments go to senior planner Jones Sorensen, jones.sorensen@bothellwa.gov. City of Bothell notice

What the city decided and what changed

Both downtowns light their trees Saturday, December 5

In Bothell, the Northshore Rotary Foundation's festival takes over Main Street at 102nd Avenue NE: a holiday market from noon to 5, activities from 4 to 6:30, and Santa arrives on a Bothell Fire…

Bothell City Hall is closed today and Friday, and so are the Bothell and Woodinville libraries

Both libraries reopen Saturday at 11 a.m.; hours by neighborhood below.

Bothell's 2027–2028 budget is adopted

The council approved it with the 2027–2033 Capital Facilities Plan last week.

Woodinville's surface water charge goes from $228 to $235 a year per house in 2027

It is in the fee schedule that came with the city's 2027–2028 budget, which passed first reading on the November 17 agenda; traffic impact fees rise 3.8% too.
